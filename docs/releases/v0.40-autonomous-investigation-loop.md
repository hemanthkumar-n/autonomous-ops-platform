# AOP v0.40 - Bounded Autonomous Investigation Loop

## Goal

Turn the enterprise `InvestigationCase` into a bounded evidence-planning loop that can decide what evidence is missing, request only explicitly registered read-only collectors, merge normalized evidence, re-evaluate confidence, and stop when the case reaches an RCA candidate or a safety/budget boundary.

## Architecture

```text
Incident / InvestigationCase
        |
        v
Confidence evaluation
        |
        +-- RCA candidate / resolved --> stop
        |
        v
Evidence gaps
        |
        v
EvidencePlanner
        |
        v
Bounded EvidenceRequest
        |
        v
Registered read-only collector
        |
        v
EvidenceCollectionResult
        |
        +-- normalized EvidenceItem
        +-- hypothesis support / contradiction
        +-- resolved evidence gaps
        |
        v
Re-evaluate case
        |
        +-- sufficient evidence --> RCA candidate
        |
        +-- insufficient evidence --> next bounded step
```

## Safety boundaries

- No arbitrary shell execution.
- No collector runs unless it is explicitly registered by name.
- Planner-generated requests are read-only by contract.
- Non-read-only requests are blocked even if supplied by another planner.
- No remediation or infrastructure mutation is implemented.
- Maximum investigation steps and total collector requests are enforced.
- Missing collectors stop cleanly instead of falling back to guessed commands.
- Collector failures are recorded in the audit timeline.

## New components

- `app/investigation/evidence_planner.py`
  - bounded `EvidenceRequest`
  - evidence-gap prioritization
  - safe collector classification
  - stop conditions when no safe next plan exists
- `app/investigation/autonomous_loop.py`
  - registered collector boundary
  - evidence merge and deduplication
  - hypothesis support/contradiction updates
  - evidence-gap resolution
  - step/request budgets
  - audit events and deterministic re-evaluation
- `app/investigation/demo_autonomous_loop.py`
  - zero-risk fixture demonstrating gap -> evidence -> RCA progression
- `tests/test_autonomous_investigation_loop.py`
  - blocking-gap priority
  - successful evidence-to-RCA flow
  - missing collector safety stop
  - request-budget stop

## Next integration steps

1. Register adapters around existing Linux memory, CPU, disk, and network investigators.
2. Add Kubernetes read-only collector adapters without exposing arbitrary `kubectl` execution.
3. Feed Prometheus evidence through a bounded query adapter.
4. Attach pattern-memory and trusted runbook retrieval to planning context.
5. Add outcome learning only after resolution validation exists.

Autonomous remediation remains intentionally out of scope.
